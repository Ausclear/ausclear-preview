NV1 Security Clearance

An NV1 (Negative Vetting Level 1) Security Clearance is a crucial requirement for individuals needing access to information classified up to the SECRET level within the Australian Government and authorised organisations.

$1,355 AGSVA Fee
Typical Processing: 3–4 months

What is an NV1 Clearance?

An NV1 (Negative Vetting Level 1) Security Clearance is a formal security assessment conducted by the Australian Government Security Vetting Agency (AGSVA) that grants you authorised access to information and resources classified as SECRET.

This level of clearance is essential for positions where you will routinely handle sensitive or classified information that is expressly not for public disclosure, and where its compromise could cause serious damage to national security. The role involves managing data and operations that require a higher level of trust and scrutiny than that provided by a Baseline clearance.

The position is formally mandated by various Australian government departments, agencies, or authorised private sector organisations working on government contracts. It demonstrates that AGSVA has conducted a thorough and ongoing assessment, concluding that you are suitable to hold such a clearance.

Eligibility Requirements

To be considered for an NV1 Security Clearance, you must satisfy the following fundamental requirements set by AGSVA:

Australian Citizenship

Mandatory. You must be an Australian citizen. This will be rigorously verified.

Checkable Background

A comprehensive and verifiable personal history spanning the last 10 years is required.

Formal Sponsorship

You must be sponsored by an Australian government department or authorised organisation. Independent applications are not accepted.

Pre-Application Employment Screening

Before your formal NV1 Security Clearance application can be submitted to AGSVA, you are typically required to undergo an initial Employment Screening Check. This preliminary screening helps to confirm your suitability for employment in a sensitive environment and usually includes:

100-Point Identification Check

Confirms your identity based on official Australian documents.

National Police Clearance

Reviews your criminal history across all Australian jurisdictions.

Right to Work Verification

Ensures you hold the necessary visas or citizenship to work in Australia.

Your employer or the sponsoring organisation will oversee and ensure these essential pre-checks are completed thoroughly before officially submitting your security clearance request to AGSVA.

Essential Documents for Your NV1 Application

Preparing the necessary documentation in advance can significantly streamline your NV1 application. You will typically need to provide several documents to verify your identity, background, and personal circumstances:

Identity & Citizenship

Full birth certificate (showing parent details), Australian citizenship certificate (if born overseas), and current photo identification such as a driver's licence or passport.

Background History

Comprehensive employment history (last 10 years, precise dates), detailed residential address history (last 10 years), and overseas travel history (last 10 years, dates and countries).

Financial & Character

Financial information (significant debts, loans, obligations) and referee contact details (personal, professional, and overseas referees where applicable).

Ensure all documents are current and accurately reflect your information to avoid delays.

The NV1 Application Process Explained

The application for an NV1 Security Clearance follows a defined pathway:

1

Sponsorship Request

Your employer or sponsoring organisation submits the formal request to AGSVA on your behalf.

2

Portal Access

You gain access to the myClearance portal. Typical timeframe: 5–10 business days from the sponsorship request.

3

Applicant Submission

You complete the application form and upload all required documents. Timeframe: 20 business days from access being granted.

4

AGSVA Vetting

AGSVA conducts comprehensive background checks and may conduct interviews. Typical timeframe: approximately 70 business days.

5

Decision & Notification

AGSVA makes a decision and your employer or sponsoring organisation notifies you of the outcome.

Transparency and accuracy throughout this process are key to a successful outcome. The overall process generally takes 3–4 months.

Pricing for NV1 Security Clearance

The base fee charged by the Australian Government Security Vetting Agency (AGSVA) for an NV1 Security Clearance is AUD 1,355 (inclusive of GST). However, who is responsible for this fee, and whether additional costs are incurred, depends on the specific circumstances of the application.

The following scenarios outline the typical cost responsibilities:

DISP-Accredited Organisations

Organisations holding current Defence Industry Security Program (DISP) accreditation that sponsor their own employees are directly responsible for the AUD 1,355 AGSVA fee. These organisations manage the ongoing governance and administrative aspects of the clearance internally, so no additional management fees are payable.

Organisations Using a Sponsoring Organisation

If an organisation lacks DISP accreditation and engages a third-party sponsoring organisation, they are responsible for all associated costs. This includes the AUD 1,355 AGSVA fee plus the separate professional fees charged by the sponsoring organisation (which can vary significantly based on services provided).

Individuals Sponsored by an Employer

For most individuals requiring a clearance for employment, the prospective or current employer covers all costs, including the AUD 1,355 AGSVA fee, as it is a requirement for the role.

Individuals Directly Engaging a Sponsor

Individuals may directly engage a third-party sponsoring organisation. In such cases, the individual is responsible for all fees, encompassing the AUD 1,355 AGSVA fee, the sponsoring organisation's service fees, and any other related expenses.

Key considerations:

Sponsoring Organisation Fees

These are distinct from AGSVA fees. Sponsoring organisations are private entities that charge for their administrative, advisory, and facilitation services. Always obtain a detailed breakdown of all fees from a sponsoring organisation before committing to their services.

Background Check Costs

Any fees associated with mandatory pre-application background checks (e.g., police checks, identity verification) are typically covered by the same party responsible for the AGSVA fee.

Applicant Time & Effort

Regardless of who covers the monetary costs, applicants must be prepared to dedicate considerable time and effort to compiling documentation and completing the application accurately.

Typical Waiting Times for NV1

Understanding the expected timeframe for an NV1 Security Clearance can help manage expectations. The process typically involves several stages:

AGSVA Application Issuance

Approximately 5–10 business days for AGSVA to officially issue the security clearance application to you after your sponsorship request is processed.

Applicant Submission Window

You are typically given 20 business days to thoroughly complete and submit your application and all supporting documents via the myClearance portal. Prompt submission is advisable.

AGSVA Vetting Process

The core vetting assessment by AGSVA usually takes around 70 business days to complete, though this can fluctuate based on complexity, caseload, and applicant responsiveness.

On average, the entire NV1 security clearance process — from initial sponsorship request to final decision — takes approximately 3–4 months. These are estimates, and individual circumstances or current AGSVA caseloads can impact actual waiting times.

What Happens if Your NV1 Clearance is Delayed or Denied?

Delays or denials in the NV1 security clearance process can occur for several reasons. Common factors include incomplete or inaccurate documentation, issues uncovered during the background check (such as undeclared financial difficulties or criminal history), or delays in obtaining responses from nominated referees.

If your security clearance is denied, you have specific rights. You will typically be provided with reasons for the adverse decision. You then have the right to request a review of the decision, usually with the guidance and support of your employer or sponsoring entity, who can assist you in understanding the appeals process.

Maintaining Your NV1 Security Clearance

Once granted, an NV1 Security Clearance generally remains valid for a period of 10 years, unless it is revoked or a higher clearance level is required. Maintaining your clearance involves ongoing responsibilities. You are obligated to report any significant changes in your personal circumstances to AGSVA — including changes in financial status, significant personal relationships, extended overseas travel, or any police involvement.

AGSVA may conduct periodic reviews of your suitability during the 10-year validity period to ensure continued eligibility and adherence to security guidelines. Adhering to these obligations is crucial for retaining your NV1 clearance and continuing to access classified information.
