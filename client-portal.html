Everything Built In

Designed for every stage

From application through to ongoing aftercare, AusClear Connect™ gives Baseline sponsored clients direct visibility and control — without the email chains, phone tag, or chasing paperwork.

Live Clearance Status

A clear, real-time view of where your clearance is in the AGSVA process — current stage, key milestones, and estimated completion timeline.

Your Details, Up to Date

View and update your contact details on file — keeping AusClear and AGSVA informed without back-and-forth emails or duplicate forms.

Overseas Travel Reporting

Submit your overseas travel reports in line with your aftercare obligations — quickly, accurately, and with a complete history available to you.

Secure Message Board

Communicate directly with the AusClear team through a secure message board — questions, document requests, and updates, all kept in one thread.

Document Centre

Store and exchange documents relevant to your engagement, accessible whenever you need them — no more digging through old email attachments.

Annual Declaration

Complete and submit your annual security declaration through the portal — a streamlined process that keeps you compliant year on year.

Bookings & Appointments

Schedule, reschedule, or cancel appointments with our team in just a few clicks — no phone tag, no missed messages.

FAQ & Help

Self-service answers to common questions, alongside direct support requests when you need a real person — both built into the portal.

Account & Security

Manage your profile, email, password, and backup codes. Two-factor authentication is mandatory for every account — biometric sign-in is available as a convenience on supported devices.

Built for Trust

Security at every layer

Security clearance information is sensitive by nature. AusClear Connect™ is built with that reality at the centre of every design decision.

Australian-hosted infrastructure
Your data stays on Australian soil — encrypted in transit with TLS and encrypted at rest.
Mandatory two-factor authentication
Every account must set up 2FA — no exceptions. Once enabled, you can choose to sign in with 2FA, or use biometrics on supported devices as a convenience. The choice is yours, the protection is non-negotiable.
Least-privilege staff access
AusClear staff access is logged, audited, and restricted on a need-to-know basis — with every action attributed to a named individual.
Aligned with PSPF and DISP requirements
Our handling, retention, and destruction protocols are aligned with the Protective Security Policy Framework and our DISP accreditation obligations.

Built to a Defence Standard

AusClear is an accredited Defence Industry Security Programme (DISP) member. The portal is built to the same standards we apply across every part of our sponsorship operation.
