Your AusClear Client Portal

Every AusClear-sponsored client gets access to Australia's first dedicated security clearance client portal — a secure command centre for your clearance journey, from application through to ongoing sponsorship and aftercare obligations.

Biometric Authentication

Enterprise-grade security with Face ID, Touch ID, and fingerprint authentication, backed by multi-factor authentication. Your sensitive clearance information is protected by the same security standards used by banks and defence contractors.

Overseas Travel Reporting

Submit and track overseas travel notifications directly through the portal — meeting your aftercare obligations with full audit trail, automatically logged against your clearance record.

Secure Messaging

Direct two-way encrypted messaging with the AusClear sponsorship team. No more chasing emails — every conversation is captured in one place, against your clearance record.

Document Management

Upload, store, and access all your clearance-related documents in one secure repository — accessible from any device, encrypted at rest and in transit.

The AusClear Client Portal is distinct from AGSVA's myClearance portal — myClearance is where you complete your vetting questionnaire; the AusClear Client Portal is where your relationship with us, your accredited sponsor, lives for the life of your clearance.
