About AusClear

Start your security clearance journey today

Practical, structured pathways to Australian Government security clearances — for individuals and Defence-industry organisations.

At AusClear, we make the security clearance process straightforward and practical. We support individuals and organisations seeking access to roles that require Australian Government security clearances, providing clear, structured pathways aligned with Defence and AGSVA requirements.

We directly sponsor Baseline security clearances under our accredited DISP membership, and facilitate NV1 and NV2 referrals through our trusted preferred clearance partner whilst our DISP Level 3 uplift progresses. Our focus is on removing uncertainty from the clearance journey and ensuring it is handled with professionalism, accuracy, and a strong commitment to compliance.

Looking Ahead

Our Future Capability

AusClear is currently undergoing DISP Level 3 uplift. Once complete, we will directly sponsor NV1 and NV2 clearances in addition to Baseline. Until then, we continue to facilitate higher-level clearances through our preferred partner, ensuring continuity of service and compliance throughout the transition.
