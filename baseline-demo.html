Baseline Security Clearance

A Baseline Security Clearance is the foundational security vetting level required for individuals needing access to government information classified as PROTECTED.

$884 AGSVA Fee
Typical Processing: 20 business days

What is Baseline Security Clearance?

Baseline Security Clearance grants access to information and resources classified as PROTECTED. This clearance is essential for roles within the Australian Government and authorised organisations that handle sensitive information, managed by the Australian Government Security Vetting Agency (AGSVA).

It is necessary for positions where:

  • Individuals require access to material that could damage national security interests if disclosed.
  • Roles in federal government agencies and authorised organisations handling PROTECTED-level sensitive information mandate such clearance.

Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for a Baseline Security Clearance, you must meet the following requirements:

Australian Citizenship

You must be an Australian citizen. Permanent residents are not eligible for a Baseline clearance.

Character Assessment

A thorough assessment of your character, honesty, and integrity will be conducted as part of the vetting process.

Sponsor Requirement

You must be sponsored by a DISP-accredited organisation or an accredited clearance sponsor such as AusClear.

Pre-Application Employment Screening

Before beginning the application process, you must complete an Employment Suitability Check. This pre-screening step ensures you meet the baseline requirements before proceeding with AGSVA vetting.

100-Point Identity Check

You must provide 100 points of identity documentation to confirm your identity before the application proceeds.

National Police Check

A current National Police Check is required as part of the pre-employment screening process.

Right to Work Verification

Confirmation of your right to work in Australia and citizenship status will be verified prior to the clearance application.

Your employer or sponsoring entity will ensure these checks are completed before submitting a clearance nomination to AGSVA.

Documents Required for Baseline Clearance

When applying for a Baseline Security Clearance, you must submit several documents to support your application. These documents verify your identity, background, and eligibility.

Birth Certificate or Passport

Primary identity documents confirming your name, date of birth and Australian citizenship.

Citizenship Documents

Proof of Australian citizenship if not born in Australia, such as a Certificate of Australian Citizenship.

Current Driver's Licence

A valid Australian driver's licence or another government-issued photo identification document.

Employment History

A comprehensive record of your employment history for the past 10 years, including contact details for previous employers.

Residential History

Details of all residential addresses for the past 5 years to support the background verification process.

Character References

Contact details for referees who can vouch for your character and integrity over the relevant period.

The Application Process

1

Sponsorship

You must be sponsored by a DISP-accredited organisation or an accredited clearance sponsor. AusClear can sponsor individuals and organisations for Baseline, NV1, and NV2 clearances.

2

Access to AGSVA Portal

Once nominated, you will receive access to the AGSVA personnel security vetting portal where you complete your detailed security questionnaire.

3

Application Submission

Complete and submit your security vetting questionnaire along with all supporting documentation. Accuracy and completeness are critical to avoid delays.

4

Vetting Assessment

AGSVA conducts background checks including identity verification, criminal history, employment verification, and character assessment.

5

Decision

Upon completion of vetting, AGSVA will issue a clearance outcome. If granted, your Baseline clearance is valid for 15 years unless circumstances change.

Fees for Baseline Clearance

The fee for applying for a Baseline Security Clearance is AUD 884 (inclusive of GST), payable to AGSVA. This is a pass-through fee — AusClear does not mark up government clearance fees.

The payment structure is as follows:

DISP-Accredited Organisations

Organisations with current DISP accreditation pay the AGSVA fee directly. No additional sponsorship fee applies if you hold your own DISP accreditation.

Organisations Using a Sponsor

Organisations without DISP accreditation that engage AusClear as their clearance sponsor are charged the AGSVA fee plus AusClear's sponsorship fee of $1,460.

Individuals (Sponsored by Employer)

For individuals whose employer holds DISP accreditation and sponsors them directly, the $884 AGSVA fee is typically borne by the employer.

Individuals (Using AusClear)

If you require AusClear to sponsor your clearance, the $884 AGSVA fee applies plus AusClear's sponsorship fee. Instalment options are available.

Key Considerations

Fee Payment Timing

The AGSVA fee is payable at the time of application submission. AusClear's sponsorship fees are invoiced separately and can be paid in full or via four instalments of $365.

Refund Policy

AGSVA fees are generally non-refundable once vetting has commenced. Please ensure you are eligible before applying.

Annual Maintenance

An annual sponsorship maintenance fee of $1,260 applies to maintain active sponsorship with AusClear after the initial year.

For cost-related enquiries, please contact AusClear at support@ausclear.com.au or call 1300 027 423.

Waiting Times

Typical waiting times for Baseline Security Clearance include:

Application Preparation

Allow 1–3 days to gather your documents and complete the security questionnaire thoroughly.

AGSVA Processing

The standard processing time is approximately 20 business days from submission for a straightforward Baseline application.

Vetting Timeline

Complex cases or additional investigations may extend processing. AusClear monitors your application status throughout.

On average, the entire process takes around 1–2 months from initial engagement with AusClear to clearance grant, depending on individual circumstances and AGSVA workload.

What Happens if Your Clearance is Delayed or Denied?

Clearances may be delayed or denied due to incomplete documentation, issues identified during the character assessment, or complex personal circumstances. In these cases AGSVA may request additional information or conduct a further assessment.

If your clearance is denied, you have the right to request a review of the decision through the AGSVA review process. AusClear can provide guidance on navigating this process.

Maintaining Your Baseline Security Clearance

Once granted, a Baseline Security Clearance remains valid for 15 years unless there is a change in circumstances that may affect your eligibility. You are obligated to report significant changes such as financial difficulties, criminal matters, or changes in foreign contacts.

AGSVA may conduct periodic reviews or updates to ensure you continue to meet the requirements of your clearance level throughout the 15-year validity period.

Conclusion

Baseline Security Clearance is essential for individuals requiring access to PROTECTED-level government information. The process requires careful preparation, accurate documentation, and the support of an accredited sponsor.

This comprehensive guide is designed to simplify the process and keep you informed at every step. AusClear is here to guide you from initial enquiry through to clearance grant.
